About RetinaGuard

RetinaGuard is an edge-deployable, offline-first screening workstation developed for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH 2026).

Production Model: EXP-001 (EfficientNet-B0 with Focal Loss, balanced sampling). Validated on 733 held-out test scans: QWK = 0.7342, Accuracy = 69.85%, Referable Sensitivity = 78.86%, Specificity = 92.64%.

Core Capabilities:

  • Image Quality Assessment & Early Rejection on Ungradable Images
  • Contrast-Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE)
  • Optic Disc, Fovea, & Retinal Vessel Segmentation
  • Microaneurysm, Disc-Excluded Hard Exudate, & Hemorrhage Candidate Detection
  • True Grad-CAM Gradient Activation Saliency Maps
  • Automated Clinical Screening Reports & Referable DR Triage
