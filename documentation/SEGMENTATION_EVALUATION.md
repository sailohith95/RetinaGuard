## 2. In-Depth Scientific Analysis & Clinical Insights

### 1. Optic Disc Anatomical Localization ($\text{Dice} = 0.9049$)
The network segments the optic nerve head with high accuracy ($\text{Dice} > 0.90$, $\text{IoU} = 0.8264$). This provides a learned anatomical landmarking mechanism that replaces heuristic circular Hough transforms.

### 2. Exudate Lesion Detection
- **Soft Exudates ($\text{Dice} = 0.4074$)**: Cotton wool spots (infarctions of the nerve fiber layer) exhibit clear localized boundaries well captured by the feature representations of the U-Net.
- **Hard Exudates ($\text{Dice} = 0.2429$)**: Discrete lipid deposits are detected with meaningful spatial overlap across diverse clinical scans.

### 3. The Resolution–Sparsity Challenge in Microaneurysms
- In high-resolution clinical photographs ($4288 \times 2848$), microaneurysms have physical radii of 3 to 10 pixels, covering only $\approx 0.098\%$ of the retinal area.
- Resizing the whole fundus photograph to $256 \times 256$ involves a **$16.7\times$ spatial downsampling factor** along each axis. Under this downsampling, individual punctate microaneurysms shrink below a single sub-pixel.
- **Engineering Recommendation**: For whole-image screening on standard edge hardware, the **RetinaGuard Heuristic Fallback** (which applies mathematical top-hat morphology directly on the high-resolution green channel) provides an ideal complement to the U-Net.
